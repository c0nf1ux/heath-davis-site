Work

Four projects that show the same pattern: find where a process breaks, then build the fix properly. Curbly stays a written case study rather than a live link, since it has no LLC or insurance behind it yet.

Curbly

An accessibility-first trash removal marketplace, designed and built end to end. It matches jobs between customers and haulers, verifies proof of service with GPS, catches fraud automatically, and passes all 260 of its tests. It scores 93 out of 100 on WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility.

Walter

An autonomous trading system running four independent strategies, paired with a civic accountability data pipeline that scrapes and cross references public records. I traced and fixed four separate production bugs at their root cause and built a parameter system that tunes itself over time.

View on GitHub

bambu-print-tools

A CLI toolkit that automates 3D printer workflows and checks mesh and print quality before anything hits the bed. I built it deliberately without MCP: no standing daemon, no lingering token overhead, just short commands that run and exit.

View on GitHub

Finch BLE Reverse Engineering

I reverse engineered a robot's undocumented BLE protocol from scratch over 40 to 60 hours, which let me bypass a required proprietary IDE entirely. The resulting system runs on an async architecture and was validated on live hardware with a full passing test suite.

About Me

Twenty years spread across property management, healthcare, retail, and banking operations roles share one thread: I find where a process breaks down and fix it permanently instead of absorbing the friction every time. I am completing a B.S. in Computer Science at SNHU, conferring January 2027, alongside a micro-credential in Foundations in AI. When I build, I reach for heavier tooling like agents, MCP servers, or frameworks only when a problem's complexity or data volume actually justifies it; most of what I ship is a plain CLI or a small service, chosen on purpose. For the last four-plus years I've served as Production Quality Coordinator in bank compliance, covering fraud prevention, AML/KYC, and payments review.
